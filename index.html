07 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
No surprises.

How does AppGuard intercept apps before they open?
AppGuard leverages native macOS accessibility and workspace notification APIs (NSWorkspace) combined with a high-performance Rust daemon to intercept window activations in under 5ms, displaying the biometric challenge before any sensitive screen content is drawn.
Does AppGuard send any data or keys to the cloud?
Never. AppGuard is strictly local-first and airgapped by design. It makes zero external network requests, contains no analytics SDKs, and stores your encrypted vault keys exclusively inside your local keychain and encrypted app state.
What happens if Touch ID fails or my Mac is in clamshell mode?
You can instantly authenticate using your encrypted Master PIN fallback or your macOS system passcode. You will never be locked out of your workstation.
Can someone bypass AppGuard by force-quitting the app in Activity Monitor?
AppGuard features built-in tamper protection. The core daemon runs as a privileged system helper with auto-restart hooks, and locked applications are immediately hidden or terminated if the security daemon is interrupted.
Is AppGuard a subscription or one-time purchase?
AppGuard is free and open-source with no recurring subscription tiers. Download the binary, install it to /Applications, and enjoy lifetime protection.
